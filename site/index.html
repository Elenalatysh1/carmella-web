The familiar problem

The problem isn’t wanting beautiful things.

It’s buying them without seeing the bigger picture.

A saved post here. A cart there. A sale that suddenly feels urgent. Another beautiful piece that works in theory, but not with the life or wardrobe you already have.

  1. i.

    Saved everywhere

    Screenshots, links, wishlists, and carts—without one place to compare what matters.

  2. ii.

    Beautiful, but difficult to wear

    The piece was right on its own. The wardrobe around it was never considered.

  3. iii.

    A full wardrobe that still feels unfinished

    More clothes do not always create more outfits.

Carmella creates a pause between desire and decision—without taking the joy out of fashion.

Questions

Good to know.

What is Carmella?{{ faqMark1 }}

Carmella is a new way to plan what you want, understand what belongs in your wardrobe, and make clearer decisions before you buy.

Is Carmella available now?{{ faqMark2 }}

Not yet. We’re building the first version now. Join the waitlist to hear about early access.

Will Carmella tell me to stop shopping?{{ faqMark3 }}

No. Carmella is not anti-shopping or about owning as little as possible. It is designed to help you choose more intentionally and enjoy what you buy more.

Do I need to upload my entire wardrobe?{{ faqMark4 }}

No. Carmella is designed to learn useful wardrobe context gradually. You can start with a few basics and add more only when it helps.

Is a fashion budget required?{{ faqMark5 }}

No. Budget is an optional clarity layer, not a restriction. The purpose is to help you see where a purchase fits in your plan.

How much will Carmella cost?{{ faqMark6 }}

Pricing will be shared closer to launch. Waitlist members will hear first about early access and launch details.

What happens after I join?{{ faqMark7 }}

You’ll receive occasional product updates and an invitation when early access becomes available.
