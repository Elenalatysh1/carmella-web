Privacy policy

How we handle your email.

Carmella is not open yet. This page covers the waitlist on this website.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we store:

  • Your email address
  • The date you joined
  • How you arrived, if your browser shares it (a referrer, or campaign tags such as utm_source)

We do not ask for a password, payment details, or your wardrobe.

Why

We use your email to tell you when early access opens and to send occasional updates about Carmella. You can unsubscribe from those emails at any time.

Who sees it

The list is kept for the Carmella team. We do not sell it. If we use a service to send email, that service only receives what it needs to deliver the message.

How long

We keep your email until you ask us to delete it, or until it is no longer needed for the waitlist.

Your choices

Write to hello@carmella.app to access, correct, or delete your email.
